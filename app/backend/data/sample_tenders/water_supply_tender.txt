TENDER NOTICE - MUNICIPAL WATER SUPPLY SCHEME

Subject: Supply and Installation of Potable Water Supply Pipeline and Quality Monitoring Network

Tender Reference No: PHED/RAJ/2026/WTR-042

1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Public Health Engineering Department invites sealed bids for the supply and laying of water distribution pipelines and water quality testing infrastructure.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 PIPING REQUIREMENTS
- Supply of High Density Polyethylene (HDPE) / Polyethylene pipes for municipal potable water supply network, PE-100 grade, working pressure PN 10, nominal diameter 110mm.
- Supply of unplasticized PVC (uPVC) pipes for cold water distribution mains with solvent cemented joints.
- All pipes shall be suitable for drinking water distribution without imparting taste or odor.

2.2 WATER QUALITY & TESTING SPECIFICATIONS
- Baseline water quality testing parameters shall comply with Indian Standards for potable drinking water quality.
- Packaged drinking water 20 litre jars for operational field staff distribution conforming to BIS packaged drinking water standards.

2.3 QUALITY CONTROL & CERTIFICATION
- All supplied pipes and fittings must carry mandatory ISI mark certification.
- Hydrostatic pressure testing of installed pipelines shall be conducted prior to commissioning.
