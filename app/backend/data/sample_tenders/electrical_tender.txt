TENDER NOTICE - ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE UPGRADE

Subject: Supply and Installation of Internal Electrical Wiring, Cables, and Energy-Efficient Lighting

Tender Reference No: PWD/ELECT/2026/089

1. SCOPE OF WORK
Turnkey execution of internal electrification for the newly constructed administrative block including cabling, power distribution boards, and luminaire fittings.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

2.1 CABLING & CONDUCTORS
- Supply of PVC insulated single-core copper conductor flexible cables for building internal wiring and lighting circuits, voltage grade up to 1100 V with flame retardant low smoke (FRLS) insulation.
- Heavy duty power cables for main distribution panel to sub-distribution boards.
- Plugs and socket-outlets 6A/16A rating conforming to relevant BIS safety specifications.

2.2 LIGHTING & LUMINAIRES
- Supply of self-ballasted 9W and 18W LED lamps for general interior office lighting, B22 and E27 cap, luminous efficacy min 100 lm/W and integrated surge protection.
- Emergency exit sign luminaires with battery backup.

2.3 SAFETY & EARTHING
- All electrical installations and equipment shall comply with Indian Standard codes of practice for electrical safety and earthing.
- Mandatory ISI marking certification required for all cables and LED lighting equipment.
